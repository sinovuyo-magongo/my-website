About Prudiey Nails

The hands, the eye, and the brand — all one person.

Prudiey Nails is a home-based nail studio run by Prudiey, a young business owner in Bloemfontein who also creates nail content online.

Her story

From home studio to online brand

Today, Prudiey is known as one of the best nail technicians in Bloemfontein. People love her creative designs, and she has a loyal group of followers online. She often shares photos and videos of her work.

She is active on social media, but until now hasn't had a website — so followers had to scroll through old posts just to see her work or figure out how to book. This site brings that all into one easy-to-find place.

Mission

To provide affordable, creative and professional nail services that are accessible to everyone, without the need for a regular high-street salon visit.

Vision

To grow Prudiey Nails from a home-based service into a recognised, professional online brand that clients feel confident recommending.
